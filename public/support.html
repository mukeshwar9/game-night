Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Game Night signs you in anonymously when you open it, and you can play everything with that. Linking a Google account (or Apple, on iPhone, when offered) is optional and keeps your name, avatar and friends if you change phone.

How do I play with a friend?

Pick a game, choose INVITE FRIEND and send the link. It opens the room in the app or the browser. You can also add friends by their six-character friend code on the Friends page.

A game will not connect, or a real-time game freezes.

Games such as Pong, Snake and Air Hockey connect the two devices directly and can fail on some mobile networks or Wi-Fi. Try Wi-Fi on both devices, or pick a turn-based game.

How do I block or report someone?

Tap the player's name in room chat, or on your Friends page, and choose BLOCK or REPORT. A blocked player cannot send you friend requests or invites and you no longer see their chat. You can unblock from Profile, under Blocked players.

I do not get invite notifications.

Turn notifications on from your Profile, and allow them in your device settings. They only tell you about game invites from friends.

The app says an update is required.

Update Game Night from the App Store or Google Play. Old versions stop working when the game changes in a way they cannot follow.

Can I buy things in the app?

No. The iPhone and Android apps have no purchases and every look is unlocked. See the Terms of Use for the website shop.

How to delete your data

  • In the app or on the website: open Profile, scroll to your account and choose “Delete my data”. It removes your profile, friends, statistics, block list and sign-in account straight away.
  • Without the app: email %CONTACT% from the address on your Google or Apple account, or tell us your friend code and the display name you used, and we will delete everything within 30 days.

The full list of what we store is in the Privacy Policy.
