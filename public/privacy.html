Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 September 2026

Game Night is a free browser game platform for playing short games with friends and strangers. This policy explains what we store when you use it, why, and how to have it deleted. We do not sell your data, show third-party ads inside the app, or use third-party analytics or advertising cookies.

Who runs Game Night

Game Night is run by its maker (“we”). You can reach us at %CONTACT% for anything in this policy, including access and deletion requests and reports of abuse.

What we store

  • An anonymous account. Opening the app signs you in anonymously and gives you a random ID. There is no password and no email unless you choose to link a Google account.
  • Your profile. The display name and avatar you pick, a friend code, and your theme and font choices. Your name and avatar are visible to other players.
  • Friends and invites. Your friends list, friend requests and game invites, and whether you are online (visible to your friends only).
  • Game rooms. The state of each room you play in (moves, scores, who sat where), your name in it, and chat messages and reactions sent in it. Other people in the room can read them. Rooms are deleted automatically after 24 hours without activity.
  • Play statistics. Your own win and loss counts, kept on your account. We also keep anonymous, aggregate counters of which games are played, with no link to you.
  • How you found us. If you arrive from an advertisement or a link, we note the campaign tags in the address (for example utm_source), the click identifier if there is one (such as fbclid or gclid) and the referring website. This is stored once on your account, and counted anonymously by day and source to measure whether advertising works. It is not shared with anyone.
  • Notifications, if you turn them on. A push token for your browser and the first 200 characters of its user agent, used only to tell you about game invites. Turn them off in your profile at any time.
  • Error reports. When the app fails we may send the error message, the page you were on, a short browser and system name, and your account ID. They are deleted after 14 days.
  • Feedback and reports. Anything you send through the feedback form or report button, with your name and account ID.
  • If you link Google. Your Google account email and name, and Google's account ID, kept by Firebase Authentication.

Direct connections in real-time games

Some real-time games (for example Pong or Snake) connect your device directly to the other player's device. To do that, both devices learn each other's IP address. Those games are not offered in the public “find an opponent” lobby; you only play them with people you invite. If you do not want another player to see your IP address, do not join real-time games.

Who processes your data

Game Night runs on Google Firebase (Authentication, Realtime Database, Hosting, Cloud Functions and Cloud Messaging), operated by Google. Data is stored on Google's servers and may be processed outside your country. We use no other service providers for storage, analytics or advertising. Advertising platforms that send you to Game Night receive whatever their own policies allow from your click; we do not send them anything from inside the app.

How long we keep it

  • Game rooms and invites: about 24 hours after the last activity.
  • Error reports: 14 days.
  • Your account, profile, friends and statistics: until you delete them.

Your choices and rights

Delete your data. In the app, open Profile and choose “Delete my data”. It removes your profile, friend code, friends, friend requests, invites, statistics and sign-in account. Chat lines you already sent in a room disappear when that room is deleted, within about a day. You can also email us and we will do it for you.

You may also ask us for a copy of your data or to correct it. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA) you may have further rights, including to object to or restrict processing and to complain to your data protection authority. We will answer within 30 days.

Children

Game Night is not directed to children under 13 and you must be at least 13 to use it (16 where local law sets that age for consent). We do not knowingly collect data from younger children. If you believe a child has used the app, email us and we will delete the account.

Safety and reporting

Chat is filtered for offensive words and you can mute or report any player from the chat. To report abuse or a privacy concern, use the report button in the app or email %CONTACT%.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Material changes will be announced in the app.
