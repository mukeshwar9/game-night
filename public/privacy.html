Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 October 2026

Game Night is a game platform for friends, free to play, in the browser and as an iPhone and an Android app. On the web it also has optional paid cosmetics and a subscription; the iPhone and Android apps have no purchases. This policy covers the website and the apps, explains what we store when you use them, why, and how to have it deleted. We do not sell your data, show third-party ads inside the app, or use third-party analytics or advertising cookies.

Who runs Game Night

Game Night is run by its maker (“we”). You can reach us at %CONTACT% for anything in this policy, including access and deletion requests and reports of abuse.

What we store

  • An anonymous account. Opening the app signs you in anonymously and gives you a random ID. There is no password and no email unless you choose to link a Google account.
  • Your profile. The display name and avatar you pick, a friend code, and your theme and font choices. Your name and avatar are visible to other players.
  • Friends and invites. Your friends list, friend requests and game invites, and whether you are online (visible to your friends only).
  • Game rooms. The state of each room you play in (moves, scores, who sat where), your name in it, and chat messages and reactions sent in it. Other people in the room can read them. Rooms are deleted automatically after 24 hours without activity.
  • Play statistics. Your own win and loss counts, kept on your account. We also keep anonymous, aggregate counters of which games are played, with no link to you.
  • How you found us. If you arrive from an advertisement or a link, we note the campaign tags in the address (for example utm_source), the click identifier if there is one (such as fbclid or gclid) and the referring website. This is stored once on your account, and counted anonymously by day and source to measure whether advertising works. It is not shared with anyone.
  • Notifications, if you turn them on. A push token for your browser or, in the apps, for your device (an Apple Push Notification service token exchanged for a Firebase Cloud Messaging token on iPhone, a Firebase Cloud Messaging token on Android), the platform it belongs to, and the first 200 characters of the user agent. It is used only to tell you about game invites. Turn notifications off in your profile at any time, or in your device settings.
  • Error reports. When the app fails we may send the error message, the page you were on, a short browser and system name, and your account ID. They are deleted after 14 days.
  • Feedback and reports. Anything you send through the feedback form or a report button (on a chat message, a profile or a drawing), with your name and account ID and the name and ID of the player you report.
  • Your block list. The players you have blocked (their account ID and the name they had), kept on your account so a block follows you to another device. Nobody else can read it, and the blocked player is not told.
  • Purchases (web only). The iPhone and Android apps do not sell anything and do not show the shop. If you buy on the website the Game Night Pass, a cosmetic pack or Supporter, we keep a record of what you own (the plan, when it renews or ends, which packs, and a payment reference). We never see or store your card number, UPI ID, bank details or billing address: our payment providers collect those (Paddle when you pay in dollars, Razorpay when you pay in rupees). We record the year you were born, once, when you first start a purchase, so we can keep purchases to players aged 13 and over.
  • If you link Google or Apple. Your Google or Apple account email and name (Apple may give us a private relay address instead of your email), and the provider's account ID, kept by Firebase Authentication. Linking is optional; without it you play with the anonymous account.

The iPhone and Android apps

The apps are the same game platform in a native shell and use the same account, rooms and data as the website. In addition:

  • They ask for permission before sending notifications, and for motion access only in the games that use tilt. You can say no and keep playing.
  • Error reports from the apps also include the app version.
  • They have no ads, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and do not use the advertising identifier. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • They have no in-app purchases. Paid cosmetics are only sold on the website, and the apps show every item as unlocked.
  • Invite links open in the app when it is installed, using the website address game-night-91464.web.app.

Direct connections in real-time games

Some real-time games (for example Pong or Snake) connect your device directly to the other player's device. To do that, both devices learn each other's IP address. Those games are not offered in the public “find an opponent” lobby; you only play them with people you invite. If you do not want another player to see your IP address, do not join real-time games.

Who processes your data

Game Night runs on Google Firebase (Authentication, Realtime Database, Hosting, Cloud Functions and Cloud Messaging), operated by Google. Data is stored on Google's servers and may be processed outside your country. For payments we use Paddle (Paddle.com Market Limited), which acts as the seller of record: it takes your payment, calculates and pays sales tax and VAT, issues receipts and handles refunds and chargebacks, and receives your email address, name, country and payment details under its own privacy policy. When you pay in Indian rupees, Razorpay (Razorpay Software Private Limited, India) processes the payment: it receives your payment details and the contact details you give it at checkout (such as phone number and email), and from us your account ID, the item you are buying and its price, under its own privacy policy. We use no other service providers for storage, analytics or advertising. Advertising platforms that send you to Game Night receive whatever their own policies allow from your click; we do not send them anything from inside the app.

How long we keep it

  • Game rooms and invites: about 24 hours after the last activity.
  • Error reports: 14 days.
  • Your account, profile, friends and statistics: until you delete them.
  • Purchase records: while your account exists. Paddle and Razorpay keep their own transaction records for as long as tax and accounting law requires, even after you delete your account; for the same reason we keep the record of each rupee payment (the order and payment reference, the item, the amount and your account ID) after your account is deleted.

Delete your data

In the app or on the website: open Profile and choose “Delete my data”. It removes your profile, friend code, friends, friend requests, invites, statistics, purchase records, birth-year answer and sign-in account, and it cancels any Game Night Pass subscription so you are not billed again. Chat lines you already sent in a room disappear when that room is deleted, within about a day. Without the app: email %CONTACT% from the address on your Google or Apple account, or tell us your friend code (shown on your Profile) and the display name you used, and we will delete the account and everything above within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm you own the account.

Your choices and rights

You may also ask us for a copy of your data or to correct it. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA) you may have further rights, including to object to or restrict processing and to complain to your data protection authority. We will answer within 30 days.

Children

Game Night is not directed to children under 13 and you must be at least 13 to use it (16 where local law sets that age for consent). Purchases are only available to players who say they are 13 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from younger children. If you believe a child has used the app, email us and we will delete the account.

Safety and reporting

Chat is filtered for offensive words. You can block any player (they can then no longer send you friend requests or invites, and you no longer see their chat) and report a chat message, a profile or a drawing. We read every report and act on reports of abuse within 24 hours: we remove the content and, where the terms are broken, the account. To report abuse or a privacy concern, use the report button in the app or email %CONTACT%.

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Material changes will be announced in the app.
