Suggested direction

Start with 01. Borrow the game cards from 02.

Keep invites contextual and prominent when present. Hide empty continuation sections. Move stats into Profile, Playground into Games, and lab/settings into secondary navigation. Keep online matchmaking visible in the play-mode chooser. Use pixel typography for short headings, readable text for descriptions, and a single bright primary action.

Desktop adaptation: a compact header and two-column home, with the primary action and resume card on the left, recent games on the right. The full catalog belongs on Games, not below an endless home feed.
