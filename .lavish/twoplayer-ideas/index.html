What Game Night can build from "2 Player Games"

JindoBlu's one-phone mini-game collection has about 120 games. Game Night already has or plans most of the famous ones. You picked six of my ten contenders. This round improves the look and feel of all six playable designs. Each game below shows a before and after, and one line on what changed.

Built: PUCK RUSH, YACHT, FACE OFF and CHOP CHOP are now real Game Night games. Each has an online room, a solo page against a bot, unit tests and a two-phone test. They are finished and waiting to be merged into the app; nothing is deployed yet. SPOT KICK and GULP stay as designs here. The phones below are still the design sketches the games were built from.

Your six picks, as they would look in Game Night

Each phone on the left is a working sketch: tap it to try the game against a random bot. The small frames beside it show other moments of the same game. Everything is drawn with the app's own colours (matcha theme), pixel font, seat cards, buttons and status pill, and the characters are real output of our avatar kit. These are design sketches, not the finished games: no sound, no networking, and the bots are random.

New in this round, across all six: every play object now has the same dark outline, a highlight and a hard shadow, so the six read as one family. Every tap answers with a press, a pop or a number flying to the score it changes. Whose turn it is and what to do next is shown where you are already looking, not only in the pill at the bottom.

Try it: tap dice to hold them, ROLL, tap a box, SCORE. Then watch Maya take her turn.

C-01YACHTBuilt

Five dice, three rolls, thirteen boxes. The whole game fits one screen with no scrolling.

2–4 playersturn-basedabout 8 minutesonline: easyeffort S/M
  1. Held dice lift and turn green. You can read your kept set without counting pips. Three small squares show how many rolls you have used.
  2. Every open box shows what this roll is worth. Brown "+12" means points on offer, grey "0" means a wasted box. The best one gets a dashed outline, so a new player is never lost.
  3. The SCORE button says what you are about to bank. "SCORE +12 · FOURS" removes the classic mis-tap.
  4. The bonus is a progress bar. 63 points in the top six boxes earns 35 more; the bar shows how close you are.
  5. Same frame as Pig. Seat cards, one play panel, two buttons and the status pill are the parts the app already has.

Round 6: what improved

The dice are now the centre of the screen: they sit in a felt tray with weight and shadow, held dice lift out of their sockets, each open box shows its score as a solid chip, and Maya's turn is played out with her own purple dice. Before, the dice were small flat squares and her turn was only a number changing.

Try it: tap a question to see how it splits the board, then ASK. Faces that cannot be the answer flip down.

C-02FACE OFFBuilt

Each player hides one of 24 faces. Ask yes/no questions, flip down the ones it cannot be, name the face first.

2 playersturn-basedabout 4 minutesonline: easyeffort M
  1. The faces are our own avatar kit. These 24 are real kit output, each with a short name. A new set is dealt every match, which fixes the "same faces every time" complaint about the app.
  2. Questions are buttons, and the game answers. It reads the answer off the secret face's real traits, so nobody can lie or misjudge, and there is no typing.
  3. Faces flip by themselves. After each answer the grid clears itself, and the big number on your seat card is how many are left.
  4. You always see your own secret face on your seat card, and how close your rival is to finding it.
  5. NAME THE FACE is the gamble. Right wins the round, wrong loses it, so guessing early with three left is a real decision.
Three left: name it or ask again?
Both secret faces are revealed

Round 6: what improved

Tapping a question now previews how it splits the board before you spend your turn: faces that would answer yes get a gold corner, the rest fade, and a line says "YES keeps 7 · NO keeps 9". Questions have icons, the answer lands as a stamp, and the cards flip down in a wave. Before, you had to count faces in your head.

Try it: tap a corner, then SHOOT! while the bar is in the green. Then you keep goal.

C-03SPOT KICKParked

Best-of-five penalties. Shooter and keeper both lock in secretly, then the kick plays out the same on both phones.

2 playerslock, then revealabout 2 minutesonline: easyeffort S/M
  1. Six corners to aim at. The dotted line is your shot; only you can see it until both players lock.
  2. The power bar adds skill. Stop it in the green and the shot is on target. Too soft is easy to save, too hard flies over the bar. The zone you are in lights up.
  3. "Last dives" makes it a mind game. The three small goals show where your rival went before, so you can read a habit or bait one.
  4. You take turns in goal. As keeper you pick a corner to dive to; the keeper wears the colour of whoever is in goal.
  5. The score is five squares each, filled for a goal and crossed for a miss, the way a shootout is shown on television.

Round 6: what improved

The goal now has depth (a back net, shadows, a bigger keeper who shuffles on the line), the aim is a target ring, and the power bar names its three zones and lights the one you are in. A badge says who is shooting. Before, the goal was flat and the bar's labels were cut off by the marker.

Try it: drag a green puck backwards and let go. Get them all through the gap.

C-04PUCK RUSHBuilt

Sling every puck through the gap to the other side. Both players fire at once; first to empty their side wins.

2 playersreal-timeabout 1 minute a roundonline: needs two-device testingeffort M
  1. It is the Air Hockey table with a wall. Same rink, same pucks, your end nearest your thumb. The app already has the physics and the peer-to-peer link.
  2. Pull back, let go. The elastic band stretches around the puck and a dotted line shows where it will fly.
  3. A puck takes the colour of the side it is on. Green ones are yours to get rid of. "4 LEFT" is the whole score.
  4. Only the gap lets pucks through. Everything else bounces back, and pucks coming the other way knock yours off line.
  5. On one phone the rink fills the screen and each player owns one end, which is how the original toy is played.
Kick-off: five pucks each
Your side is clear: round won

Round 6: what improved

Each half is tinted in its owner's colour, so "green pucks are yours" needs no explaining. Pucks, wall and bands have bevels and shadows, arrows mark the gap as the only way through, and the score is a row of pucks that empties. Pucks spark when they cross or hit the wall. Before, it was flat discs on a grey field and the hint was cut off.

Try it: tap LEFT or RIGHT (or the arrow keys). The red button is the side with a beam.

C-06CHOP CHOPBuilt

A 30-second race. Knock crates out of the stack as fast as you can without a beam landing on your head.

2–8 playerssimultaneous race30 secondsonline: easyeffort S
  1. It is you with the mallet. The character is the full-body avatar from your profile, so the game needs no hero art of its own.
  2. Two buttons. Tap a side to hop there and smash the bottom crate. The stack drops by one.
  3. Striped beams are the danger. If one comes down on your side you are stunned for a second and lose your streak. You are never knocked out, so a bad start can still win.
  4. Everyone gets the same stack. It is a fair race: same crates, same beams, most crates in 30 seconds.
  5. You can see the race. A chip in the corner shows your rival's face and how far ahead or behind you are, and it changes colour when the lead changes.
Hit by a beam: one second stunned
Time up

Round 6: what improved

The crates and mallet are redrawn so they read at a glance, the clock sits on the timer bar, your lead over Maya is one chip, and the button on the side with a beam turns red. That last one teaches the rule through the controls; in the real game it should fade after the first rounds, or the race is too easy. Before, the lead was two small faces on an unlabelled rail.

Try it: press GULP! when the button says NOW!. Hold off when it warns of a wasp.

C-10GULPParked

Four frogs round a pond, one button each. Catch fireflies with your tongue; the most points in 30 seconds wins.

2–4 playersreal-time30 secondsonline: 4-player real-time is neweffort M
  1. Each player is a frog in their seat colour on a lily pad. Yours is always at the bottom of your own screen.
  2. One button. GULP! fires your tongue straight down your lit lane and grabs the first thing inside it.
  3. Three kinds of bug. A firefly is +1, a gold one is +3, a red wasp is −2. Wasps drift in front of the good ones, so timing beats mashing.
  4. The button recharges. The bar inside it refills in about half a second after each gulp.
  5. Frogs, not hippos. The marble-grabbing toy this comes from is a trademark, so the theme, the creatures and the scoring are our own.
One phone: a button in each corner
Time up

Round 6: what improved

The frogs are redrawn to read as frogs, and your lane is now a lit strip that is the exact catch zone. The button tells you what a press would grab right now: "NOW!", "GOLD! NOW!" or "WASP FIRST · HOLD!". The pond's rim drains as the timer and fireflies glow. Before, you had to guess whether a bug was close enough to the dotted line.

What players say about the app

They love

  • Works with no internet, on one phone, in a car or a queue.
  • A bot with three levels, so you can play alone.
  • Simple flat art and a new game every few weeks.
  • A "cup" that strings seven random games into one contest.

They complain about

  • Ads after nearly every match, some unsuitable for children; games moved behind a paywall.
  • Bots that seem to cheat: Ludo dice, Backgammon, Memory on hard.
  • Rules that are wrong or unexplained: Chess, Pool, Basketball's first-shooter advantage.
  • Only a handful of games can be played online.

They ask for

  • an option of inviting friends … who are far away from us — the most repeated request.
  • Three and four players.
  • Pick-your-own tournament: select which 7 games to play.
  • Fresh faces in Guess the Person.
