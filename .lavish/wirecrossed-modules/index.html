◇ MOCK ENDLESS CIRCUIT

The Endless-mode module (level 16+): every wire carries three attributes — LED, ★ sticker, stripe. Handbook's grid answers CUT / SKIP / CUT IF… per wire. T2 adds more shell-keyed letters. Endless = procedural budget (+2/bomb), clock floor 2:15.
