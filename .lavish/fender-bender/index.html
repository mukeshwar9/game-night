WORKING NAME

FENDER BENDER

A 30-second elbows-out road brawl. Everyone drives up the same crowded flyover. Shove a rival into a bus or off the edge, and be the last car still rolling.

2–4 PLAYERS · ONE PHONESOLO VS BOTSONLINE 2P 30–45 S ROUNDSCATEGORY: REFLEXEFFORT: MEDIUM

WHAT THE SOURCE DOES

Top-down road, one virtual joystick per player, traffic sweeps through, three crashes or a fall into the water and you are out. One round, last car wins.

WHAT WE CHANGE

Portrait phone with big thumb pads off the road, a road that closes in so no round stalls, a HORN shove, and knocked-out players come back as a truck.

WHAT I NEED FROM YOU

Four calls at the bottom: build it or not, the name, the player counts for the first version, and which twists ship first. Jump to decisions.

WHAT CHANGED SINCE YOUR FIRST LOOK

1 · LOOKS REAL

Shaded, glossy vehicles with soft shadows. Textured road, street lamps, moving water with boats. Pick a dark theme for headlights and lamp glow.

2 · MOVES AND SOUNDS

Bots play behind the PLAY button. Cars roll in, squash on impact and fishtail when rammed. Smoke, fire, explosions, splashes. Sound effects, and a buzz on phones.

4 · FOLLOWS THE THEME

Use the THEME menu at the top. Road, water, traffic, lights and warnings are mixed from the chosen theme's own colours. Sixteen of the app's themes are loaded here.

3 · RAMMING WORKS

The car driving in harder wins the bump. The loser is knocked about a lane and skids with almost no steering, so you can send them off the edge or into a bus.

SEATS

CONTROLS

  • Touch: put a thumb on your coloured pad and drag. The stick appears where you touch. A quick tap on the pad sounds the HORN.
  • Keyboard, P1: ← ↑ → ↓ steer, Space horn. P2 (all-human mode): W A S D, F horn.
  • Ram to shove: drive into a rival at speed. The car driving in harder wins the bump; the other is knocked away and skids for a moment with almost no steering. Aim them at the edge or at a bus.
  • Three hits from traffic wrecks you. Leave the road and you are out. A bump from a rival costs no heart by itself.
  • After 28 seconds the road closes in from both sides.

TWISTS · SWITCH ON AND REPLAY

Everything is drawn and synthesised in code, no image or sound files: shaded vehicles with soft shadows, lamp posts, boats, water, smoke, sparks, skid marks, explosions, splashes, and sound effects. Bots play behind the PLAY panel. Prototype limit: simple bots.

HOW THE SOURCE GAME PLAYS

A redrawn schematic, not a screenshot. The source is a landscape screen: road through the middle, water above and below, one joystick per player in the corners with a small counter beside it.

THE LOOP

  1. Everyone's car starts mid-road. Steer anywhere with the stick.
  2. Cars, box trucks and long buses pass through the screen.
  3. Three crashes and the car explodes. Touch the water and you lose at once.
  4. With four players, every car still alive gains a point each time a rival goes out. Last car alive tops the podium.

FUN

Readable in one glance. A round is about half a minute. The best moment is ramming a friend into a bus.

FRUSTRATING

Knocked-out players sit and wait. The store's top review calls the joysticks too small. Hiding in a corner and waiting looks like a workable strategy.

THE GAME NIGHT VERSION

THREE WAYS OUT

  1. Each car has three hearts. A hit from traffic costs one and gives a second of flashing safety.
  2. Cross the edge of the flyover and you are out, whatever your hearts.
  3. Rivals shove each other for free. The damage comes from where you send them.
  4. From 28 s the road narrows steadily, so a round cannot stall.

WHERE EVERYONE SITS · ONE PHONE

The phone lies flat. Pads sit outside the road, so no thumb covers a car. The stick floats: it starts wherever the thumb lands, which answers the source's "joystick too small" complaint. Four players on a small phone is tight (each pad is about 180 px wide), so tablets are the sweet spot for four.

MATCH

Two players: first to three round wins, the app's standard. Three or four: you score one point for every rival you outlast in a round; first to 5 (three players) or 7 (four) takes the match. Placings feed the night scoreboard.

CLOSEST GAMES WE HAVE

Sumo Arena is the nearest: shove them off a shrinking platform. Tron and Snake Battle share "do not crash". None has a moving hazard field, hit points, or more than two players at once, so this does not duplicate them.

EDGE CASES

Last two out in the same frame: a draw, both get the same points. A car pinned against the bottom by a bus slides sideways, never through it. A finger that slides off its pad keeps steering until lifted. Online: if the guest drops, the standard WAIT / CLAIM WIN applies.

FIVE TWISTS

SHIP FIRSTCOST: SMALLPLAYABLE ABOVE

1 · HORN

Change: a tap blasts cars near you outward, then recharges for four seconds. Why: the source has only steering; this adds one timing decision and a way to punish tailgaters. Risk: spam near an edge feels cheap, so the recharge has to be visible.

SHIP FIRSTCOST: SMALL–MEDIUMPLAYABLE ABOVE

2 · GHOST TRUCK

Change: two seconds after going out, your pad drives a truck that keeps coming down the road. Why: fixes the worst part of any elimination game with three or four players: nobody waits. It also drags the leader back. Risk: ganging up on one survivor; the truck is slow to steer to keep it fair.

SECOND WAVECOST: MEDIUMPLAYABLE ABOVE

3 · ROADWORKS

Change: telegraphed events: a lane closes for a few seconds, or an oil slick drifts through and steering goes soft. Why: every round gets a different shape, and a closure next to a rival is a shove waiting to happen. Risk: more to read on a small screen; needs a clear two-second warning.

SECOND WAVECOST: MEDIUM

4 · DISPATCHER · ONE VS ALL

Change: one player has four lane buttons and a refilling budget, and sends the traffic. The rest survive. The role rotates each round; you score for how long the others last on your shift. Why: an asymmetric party mode nothing else in the app has, and it works with only two players. Risk: needs its own balance pass.

LATERCOST: MEDIUM–LARGE

5 · CONVOY · CO-OP

Change: everyone guards one slow van that drives itself. Shoulder traffic out of its path; score is distance. A daily seed gives the same road to everyone. Why: the app marks co-op games and has a daily puzzle slot, and this turns the same driving into teamwork. Risk: the most new content of the five.

RECOMMENDATION

Ship the base game with HORN and GHOST TRUCK. They are the cheapest two, and they fix the source's two real weaknesses: only one verb, and dead time after a knockout.

ROADWORKS next, once the base feel is tuned. DISPATCHER and CONVOY are separate modes that reuse the same simulation; decide on them after people have played the first version.
